How to Add to Apple Mail:
  1. Fill in details and click "Copy Signature to Clipboard".
  2. In Apple Mail, open Settings > Signatures and click +.
  3. Select all (Cmd + A), delete default text, and paste (Cmd + V).
  4. Uncheck "Always match my default message font" at the bottom.
Note: Images show as blank boxes in Settings, but load properly in new emails.
